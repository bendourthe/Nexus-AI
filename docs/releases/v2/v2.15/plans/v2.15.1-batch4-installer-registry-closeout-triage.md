## Disposition contract

- Resolve only after checking the present implementation and running evidence appropriate to the original claim. Cite the proving command and observed result.
- Supersede only when the later identity preserves every outstanding obligation. Compare bodies and keep unique requirements. The destination must remain visible and open when work remains.
- Owner-only closure requires an explicit recorded decision. Silence is not acceptance.
- A valid unresolved item may be carried with its original body, provenance, specific current reason, concrete next proving step and verified destination. Preparing a proposed migration is reversible; applying a signed approval class requires its actual coverage. A copied entry is still owed.
- Unproven work remains open. If destination/authority is unavailable, this plan stays incomplete. Do not change a cutoff, start_head, approved scope or frozen acceptance gate to obtain a green verdict.
- Stop after three unsuccessful repair attempts on the same failure, record its root cause, then continue independent work. Do not repeat full suites without a changed concern.

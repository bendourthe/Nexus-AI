## Plan delta

**Disposition**: Incomplete (not blocking).

**Evidence**: the checker's parse at the pin (`f1689e72b6f96835`) matched the plan's baseline exactly (346 problems, 210 open in scope plus 16 in v2.15). It also showed three things the plan does not say:

1. The v2.5 to v2.11 ledgers are earlier snapshots of the carry-forward register. Of their 151 unreadable rows, 120 copy an entry recorded in an earlier ledger, for example `BG-2` from v2.4. The plan says to rewrite tables into headings. Doing that to the copies would add 120 open entries that repeat recorded items, against the Goal's "duplicate-free".
2. Every in-scope problem is a row problem. The 140 `heading-without-id` problems are all in the legacy-cutoff ledgers, so T001's decision 5 (ids for id-less headings in v2.5 to v2.11) has nothing to act on.
3. Rows were never items to the checker, so Phase 2's "open counts equal to the pre-conversion counts" has no before value for them.

**Resolution in this phase** (decisions 1, 7 and 8 in [the decision record](../v2.15.0-ledger-conversion-decisions.md)): a row that copies an entry recorded elsewhere stays in its table and gains that ledger's `vX.Y#` prefix. Only a row that is the item's sole record becomes an entry. The before count is defined as parsed open items plus original open rows.

**Consequence for remaining phases**:

- Phase 2 checks open counts against decision 8, not against the parsed count alone.
- Phase 4's superseded markers also apply to the snapshot originals the register carries: v2.6's 8 entries, v2.11's 16 and v2.5's `CI-v251-1`. These are not only v2.4 and earlier items.
- Phase 3's triage must resolve the register's `DF-v211-4`, `DF-v211-5` and `WN-v211-5` against the release ledger's Resolved table (decision 4).

**Queue re-assessment (plan entry and Phase 1 entry)**: no drift. `check_plan_completion.py members v2.15` lists only v2.15.0, and no other `Status: queued` plan exists under `docs/`.

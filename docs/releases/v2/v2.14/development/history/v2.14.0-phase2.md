## Plan delta

**Disposition**: Incomplete (blocking for gate V-1, resolved by owner decision).

**Evidence**: the plan located the voice delay in synthesis and delivery. Pass 1 of the streamed pipeline (built as planned) cut time to first audio from 38.6 s to 19.6 s but still missed V-1, and direct Ollama timing showed the chat model's hidden reasoning delays its first visible token by 16 to 21 s, plus per-call Whisper construction of 4 to 7 s. Neither is in the plan's Phase 2 subtasks. Two further defects outside the plan surfaced and were fixed because voice could not work without them: Windows blocks a worker's first native-module import while the reader waits on the stdin pipe (synthesis hung), and the sidecar's exit handler reported streams as generic errors.

**Owner decisions (2026-10-16)**: (1) stay on Opus 5.5 for the phase; (2) for the one rework iteration, apply both levers, Whisper caching and `think: false` on voice turns; (3) after the rework still missed V-1 (3,990 ms), keep the streamed path instead of reverting it as the cut-line says, and record the miss as a known gap.

**Consequence for remaining phases**: Phase 3 and 4 are unaffected; the reasoning splitter (T007) and `reasoningOpensInPrompt` hook exist for T012. Phase 5 must: carry the cut-line departure into the Goal-vs-codebase review (the Goal's 2.0 s clause is not met); include the Windows-only stdin test in its CI comparison (it skips on Linux runners); run the packaged-app listening checks as human testing; and add the new voice pins to `runtimes/audio/requirements.txt` for pip-audit. The two unfixable-in-run items are in `docs/releases/v2/v2.15/known-gaps.md` because gaps opened during the run cannot migrate at the v2.14 close.

**Queue re-assessment (phase entry)**: at Phase 2 entry no other plan was queued.

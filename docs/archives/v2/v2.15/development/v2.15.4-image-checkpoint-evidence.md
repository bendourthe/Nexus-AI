## Behavior

The sessions.db owner now retains immutable approval-attempt checkpoints in the owning turn/session transaction. Each checkpoint preserves the source hash, complete validated recipe, cursor and redo tail, request/Ask identities, private attempt ID, caller/run and capture time. Revision-zero capture leaves the live recipe at zero. Duplicate processing of an approved Ask cannot create another snapshot. Distinct attempts and approvals remain distinct; no checkpoint eviction or original-file deletion was added.

Actual approval replays permission policy and checks the live waiter, synchronously captures SQLite recovery state, then persists the Ask JSON decision before resolving the waiter. These are two separate stores, not one atomic transaction. Checkpoint or JSON-save failure grants no access and leaves a safe pending retry. A later click creates a fresh attempt and snapshot, including viewer edits made after the failed save. An unused checkpoint from a failed attempt is recovery data, never permission.

Only the viewer grant can list or restore its exact image's checkpoints. Listing returns at most 50 metadata records per page, and a restore looks up one scoped checkpoint instead of loading all recipe history. Restore validates the current PNG/source identity and saved recipe, uses current-revision compare-and-swap, and commits at current revision plus one. The source and immutable checkpoint are unchanged. Successful recovery invalidates pending/prepared approvals and issued agent write/render/replay grants through an image generation barrier. Approval persistence and preview/export rendering recheck authority after their awaited work; a delayed completion cannot issue access, return PNG bytes or write an export after recovery.

The viewer uses the production client, flushes pending edits, shows checkpoint metadata, confirms replacement and reports saved/conflict/error states. Older hosts without the optional client capabilities remain usable without presenting recovery authority. Image-only deletion dialogs disclose that history, saved edits and checkpoints are deleted while shared original images remain on disk; Chat and Video copy remains unchanged.

## Dispositions

`Prove resolved` identifies a candidate supported by current source that still needs the named fresh verification. `Supersede` identifies an exact later identity that must retain the original obligations. `Implement` identifies concrete missing behavior or tests. `Carry` retains a conditional decision, evidence hold, or unresolved design boundary, with a specific next step. Standing rules and deliberately retained components should receive an explicit documented disposition rather than speculative feature work.

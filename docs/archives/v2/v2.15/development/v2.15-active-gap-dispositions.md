## Classification rules

- **Required implementation**: an observed defect or missing mechanism in behavior already shipped or explicitly accepted. Keep it open until the expected behavior is implemented and proved, or a specific owner-approved deferral identifies exactly what remains. Copy verification is not behavior verification.
- **Required qualification**: a named acceptance observation for shipped behavior is missing. Keep its original evidence bar, including real hardware, installed app, process boundary or exact artifact identity. A component test does not replace it.
- **Optional candidate/watch**: a proposal is conditional or deliberately not admitted; retaining its original trigger is an accountable outcome. It must not become a required new model/runtime subsystem merely because an older ledger lists it.
- **Historical limitation**: a past run or environment limitation cannot be changed retroactively. Preserve the fact and original evidence. Close/supersede only against the original rule and any required explicit owner acceptance.
- **Owner decision**: the unresolved choice changes retention, history, model admission, user-facing scope, trust boundaries or repository protection. Prepare the options and recommendation, but do not record a choice or fabricate maintainer M5 usage.

Each original identity appears exactly once in the following tables. Mixed entries identify their separate sub-obligations without pretending that one small fix resolves the whole item. BG-2, QG-6 and WN-19 are already resolved in the current ledger. Other source-marker changes below remain proposals until applied from their exact evidence.

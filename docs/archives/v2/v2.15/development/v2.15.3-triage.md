## Acceptance rules

Required implementation means a production or deterministic-check obligation remains to be implemented or verified. Required qualification means the original claim needs observation through its real boundary; source reads, jsdom, mocks and a build do not establish that observation. Both remain named archive blockers until their proof or an explicit approved change to the obligation exists. A deferral preserves debt and does not implement behavior.

Optional candidate/watch applies only where the original body makes work conditional or advisory. Its trigger and truthful support limit remain visible. Historical limit records a missing old measurement or artifact provenance without inventing it. Owner decision identifies the original explicit choice; silence is not acceptance. These classifications are proposals, and the original source body remains authoritative if review identifies another required clause.

The Original rule cell preserves the selected original Exit condition, Next step, Suggested next step, Owner / next evidence or Resolved wording verbatim apart from Markdown/HTML table escaping. Current evidence was inspected during the source audit; recheck concurrent worktree changes before applying any candidate. Exact later identities are proposed supersession destinations only. Unique original requirements must survive there before any supersession is applied.

## Overview

The comparison did not establish that the existing local model fails the rewriting job. This narrow experiment measures a single-request adaptation before another model download or runtime is justified. It does not claim to execute or qualify the installed skill's full tool-assisted workflow. It delivers a small development evaluator, synthetic fixtures, immutable outputs and a decision record. It creates no Chat control, automatic answer postprocessor, agent tool or rewrite service.

Three phases freeze the evaluation, collect baseline/compatibility evidence, then complete semantic review and final integration. The greatest risk is interpretation of quality: literal retention cannot establish unchanged meaning, and a language remains HOLD without a qualified reviewer. Final-phase human evaluation is required measurement, not an informal impression. Earlier outputs remain explicitly unreviewed.

H3 is a feasibility decision in this patch. Current Nexus clients do not express all Humanizer controls. A live Humanizer trial, adapter work, model provisioning or catalog admission is a future scope decision. The plan stops at the comparison's conditional gate instead of assuming a second model is necessary.

## Goals First / Definition of Done

- **Problem and persona**: the solo builder and maintainer in [STRATEGY.md](../../../../../STRATEGY.md) lack local evidence about the existing rewrite path on this workload. Measure resource behavior against M2 and support the M5 decision about whether another capability is useful. This does not claim an improved shipped feature or a completed real-use target.
- Freeze 4 pilot and 16 held-out synthetic drafts: 2 languages x 4 genres x 2 held-out drafts, covering email, essay, report and post writing. Freeze source propositions, exact literal obligations and document structure independently of generated output.
- Produce one scored baseline output per held-out item after one pilot pass. No quality retry, cherry-picked sample, hidden exclusion or post-result threshold change. The denominator remains 16 even when calls fail; an incomplete run is HOLD.
- Approved screening gate per language: all eight outputs are complete and meet exact/structural obligations with zero changed/added/omitted factual propositions. Four drafts deliberately need editing and four are clean controls, one of each per genre. Freeze an anchored readability scale (1 unusable, 2 major repair needed, 3 readable with awkwardness, 4 natural, 5 polished). Every edit-needed output must reach at least 4, improve at least one point from its source, and meet its source-specific editing goal; every clean control must preserve voice and avoid readability regression. An unchanged clean control may pass; an unchanged edit-needed draft may not. A locally recorded reviewer self-attests proficiency in professional writing and checking meaning in each reviewed language. Qualify only completed reviews; unavailable or unreviewed languages are HOLD. This strict small-sample rule is not a population error estimate or universal guarantee.
- Keep raw synthetic outputs, exact request/skill/model/runtime identities and keyed reviews. Do not commit private drafts, installed skill bodies, credentials, local machine paths or conversation data.
- Record Humanizer support for exact prompt/template, sampler controls, EOS/output bounds, context and cancellation. Unknown or unsupported required controls yield HOLD. No untyped options, ad-hoc HTTP workaround, extra runtime or production-client edit is allowed.
- Exercise the real CLI in validation/scoring modes and against the installed model. Controlled adapters and validation-only mode cannot establish live inference, memory fit or output quality.
- Finish the mandatory final evidence duties, independent Goal review and local gates before an approved implementation-branch publication. Release work is outside this plan.
